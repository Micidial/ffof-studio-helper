FFOF Studio Helper

Uses YouTube API Services

FFOF Studio Helper is a private desktop tool made and used only by its owner, Matteo Nuzio, to manage his own YouTube channels: Fun Facts Over Feelings, Toon Bad, Hushed Paranormal, Bitstory and A Terrible Time to Be Alive.

What it does

  • Uploads short videos that the owner made himself to his own channels.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, category, playlist and publish time.
  • Reads the owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, audience) to plan new videos.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It has no public sign-up and is not offered to other people. Each channel is connected through Google's standard sign-in (OAuth 2.0), and access can be removed at any time from Google security settings.

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data is handled as described in our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
