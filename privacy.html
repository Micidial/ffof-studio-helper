Privacy Policy

Uses YouTube API Services

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Shorts Publisher ("the tool") uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by its owner, Matteo Nuzio, on YouTube channels he owns. It does not collect data about any other person.

What data the tool accesses

  • Basic information about the owner's channels (channel ID, title).
  • Information about the owner's videos and playlists (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, schedule).
  • The owner's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience country, age and gender in aggregate).

How the data is used

Only to upload and update the owner's videos and to review how they perform. The data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How the data is stored

Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Analytics results are kept only as long as needed to review a video and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. No data is stored on any server.

Cookies

The tool and this website do not use cookies and do not place any information on your device.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time through Google security settings. When access is removed, the stored sign-in tokens and any stored API data are deleted within 7 days.
